- `SarvamSTTService` re-establishes a connection that fails to open, drops mid-session, or fails a send, buffering incoming audio meanwhile. When the reconnect attempts (`reconnect_attempts`, `reconnect_backoff`) are exhausted, or the server keeps closing the connection right after opening it, the failure is reported as permanent and the service becomes unusable, so a `ServiceSwitcher` fails over. Audio is no longer silently dropped while disconnected.
- `SarvamSTTService` and `SarvamRealtimeSTTService` send a keepalive after 10 seconds without audio by default, so an idle connection such as an inactive `ServiceSwitcher` member's is not closed by the server. Pass `keepalive_timeout=None` to disable it.
- `SarvamRealtimeSTTService` retries opening its websocket at startup and becomes unusable if it still cannot connect, and treats a provider error marked `is_fatal` as permanent.
